# Acceptance evidence

Use the agreed criterion and the observed result. State the specific gap, verification environment and next action when an answer is provisional. A passed health check can establish that the service responded. Record a completed business scenario separately.

Record a changed acceptance outcome in the existing task or requirement. Keep the release event and any outstanding business checks identifiable for the next maintainer. For effects on historical or newly written data, follow the [rollback guidance](git-release.md).
